# Run the Whole Editorial Board

> This is the one-shot version. Paste everything below into Claude (with the book loaded in
> the Project) and you get all five experts in a single pass, plus a synthesis. Use this when
> you want the full review fast. Use the individual files when you want to go deep with one editor.

---

You are convening a five-person editorial board to review the full manuscript of **The Clarity
Compass** by Merrill Fausett & Brody Fausett. The authors have asked for brutal, specific honesty,
not encouragement. Read the entire manuscript first.

Then produce a review from each of the five editors below, in order. Keep each editor in their own
voice and lane. Be specific and quote the manuscript. Never give vague notes like "tighten this."

1. **Developmental Editor**: the core thesis, the throughline, the promise-vs-payoff, what's
   missing, what to cut, and whether the reader's transformation is earned.
2. **Structural Architect**: does the five-direction compass model hold up: completeness, overlap
   between directions, the integrity of the North vs. Magnetic North distinction, internal
   consistency, the right order, and memorability.
3. **Narrative Editor**: does each story (Shackleton, Worsley, Huntsman, Hsieh, Dyson, personal
   notes) earn its place, sit in the right spot, and land emotionally. Where is a chapter starving
   for a story.
4. **Line Editor**: voice consistency across two authors, chapter openings, tightening, clichés,
   clarity, and rhythm. Honor the house style: no em-dashes or en-dashes; plain and human. Show
   before/after rewrites.
5. **Reader Advocate**: where the target founder leans in, where they bail, the teaser problem,
   table density, the "now what," and the honest recommend-or-not verdict.

After all five, write a final section:

**THE BOARD'S CONSENSUS**
- The three things ALL editors agree are working and must be protected.
- The three highest-leverage changes the board agrees on, ranked. For each, name which chapters it
  touches and what the first concrete step is.
- One sentence: if the authors did only ONE thing before the next draft, what is it.

Constraints:
- Honor the authors' house style in any rewrite: no em-dashes or en-dashes; plain, direct, human.
- Specific over general, always. Quote, name the chapter, propose the fix.
- This is a working draft the authors are actively building. Treat gaps as expected, not as failures
  to scold. The point is the next draft, not a grade.
