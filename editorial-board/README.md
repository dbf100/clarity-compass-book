# The Editorial Board

Your on-call panel of book editors. Each file is a ready-to-use prompt. Open it, copy everything
below the line, paste it into a Claude chat that has the book loaded, and Claude becomes that
editor and reviews the manuscript.

| Editor | What they do | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `developmental-editor.md` | Big-picture: thesis, throughline, what's missing, what to cut | You want to know if the whole book is working |
| `structural-architect.md` | Pressure-tests the five-direction compass framework | You want to know if the model holds up |
| `narrative-editor.md` | Stories and examples: are they earning their place | A chapter feels flat or a story feels long |
| `line-editor.md` | Sentences: voice, tightening, clarity, clichés | You want sharper prose without losing your voice |
| `reader-advocate.md` | The target reader's honest reaction | You want to know where a real founder bails |
| `run-the-board.md` | All five at once, plus a consensus | You want the full review in one pass |
| `_FINAL-publish-polish.md` | ⚠️ Gated final pass: typos, grammar, AI-slop, consistency | ONLY when the book is content-complete and ready to be made publish-ready. Not part of the board. |

Every editor is instructed to be brutally honest and specific, to quote the manuscript, and to
protect what's working before naming what isn't. They all honor the house style: no em-dashes,
plain and human.

**Tip:** go deep with one editor at a time when you're working on a specific chapter. Use
`run-the-board.md` when you want the high-level "what matters most next" picture.
